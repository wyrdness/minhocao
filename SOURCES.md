# Minhocão Sources

A comprehensive bibliography and resource guide for researching the Minhocão cryptid of southern Brazil, including historical documents, scientific investigations, geological studies, folkloric research, and comparative mythology.

---

## Primary Historical Documents

### Fritz Müller's Investigation (1877-1878)

1. **Müller, Fritz (1877)**  
   "Ein noch unbeschriebener Wurm"  
   *Zoologischer Anzeiger*, Band 1  
   Original German report of Müller's investigation into the Minhocão phenomenon. Contains witness interviews and physical evidence documentation.

2. **Müller, Fritz (1878)**  
   "The Minhocão of Southern Brazil"  
   *The Journal of the Royal Microscopical Society*  
   English translation and discussion of Müller's findings, introducing the phenomenon to British scientific community.

3. **Anonymous (1878)**  
   "A Gigantic Earthworm in Brazil"  
   *Nature*, Vol. 18, pp. 628-629  
   Contemporary scientific discussion of Müller's report, including skeptical commentary and requests for further evidence.

4. **Darwin, Charles (1881)**  
   *The Formation of Vegetable Mould through the Action of Worms*  
   John Murray, London  
   While not directly about the Minhocão, Darwin's work on earthworms provided context for Müller's speculation about giant annelids.

5. **Benham, W.B. (1890)**  
   "An Attempt to Classify Earthworms"  
   *Quarterly Journal of Microscopical Science*, Vol. 31  
   Discussion of earthworm taxonomy that occasionally references the Minhocão as a possible giant annelid.

### 19th Century Brazilian Sources

6. **O Despertador (1878)**  
   "O Minhocão nas Terras de Lages"  
   Lages, Santa Catarina newspaper  
   Local newspaper accounts of sightings and trench formations in the Lages region.

7. **Província do Paraná (1882)**  
   "Fenômeno Estranho em São Joaquim"  
   Curitiba, Paraná newspaper  
   Report on the São Joaquim sighting by gauchos.

8. **Ave-Lallemant, Robert (1858)**  
   *Viagem pelo Sul do Brasil no Ano de 1858*  
   Original German edition  
   German traveler's account of southern Brazil, including indigenous legends of giant serpents.

9. **Saint-Hilaire, Auguste de (1887)**  
   *Viagem a Curitiba e Santa Catarina*  
   French naturalist's observations of southern Brazil, documenting local folklore.

10. **Silveira, Valdomiro (1894)**  
    "Lendas e Superstições do Sul"  
    *Revista Brasileira*, Rio de Janeiro  
    Early compilation of southern Brazilian folklore including Minhocão accounts.

### Early 20th Century Documentation

11. **Roquette-Pinto, Edgar (1912)**  
    "Notas sobre o Folklore do Brasil Meridional"  
    *Revista do Instituto Histórico e Geográfico Brasileiro*  
    Ethnographic study including Minhocão traditions.

12. **Magalhães, Basílio de (1913)**  
    *O Folk-Lore no Brasil*  
    Livraria Quaresma, Rio de Janeiro  
    Early Brazilian folklore compilation with section on legendary creatures.

13. **Lima, J. F. de Oliveira (1923)**  
    "Relato do Avistamento em Irati"  
    Local parish records, Irati, Paraná  
    Priest's documentation of the 1923 Irati observation.

14. **Cascudo, Luís da Câmara (1954)**  
    *Dicionário do Folclore Brasileiro*  
    Ministério da Educação e Cultura, Rio de Janeiro  
    Definitive Brazilian folklore encyclopedia with Minhocão entry.

15. **Cascudo, Luís da Câmara (1976)**  
    *Geografia dos Mitos Brasileiros*  
    Global Editora, São Paulo  
    Comprehensive study of Brazilian mythological geography, extensive Minhocão section.
